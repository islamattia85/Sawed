# Data protection impact assessment: Peakless

Peakless Energy Limited (being formed), the controller. Written 29 October 2026 for the private test, to be reviewed before the public launch. Approved by: ____________ (director), date ________.

**Conclusion.** The private test can go ahead. With accounts switched off, nothing about a household reaches our database: the risks that remain are low and covered below. Before the public launch, the four items under "Before the public launch" must be done.

## What we do and why

Peakless works out what each Irish electricity plan would cost a home, and whether solar panels or a battery would pay back. People answer questions about their home and may load the meter file they download from ESB Networks. We write this assessment because half-hourly meter readings can show when a home is busy or empty. Whether the law requires one at this scale is for the solicitor to confirm; it is good practice either way.

## The data

| What | Where it is | Who can see it | Kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| Answers about the home, results, meter readings | The person's own browser | Only them | Until they clear it |
| Account: email, name, the home, its meter readings (only when accounts are on) | Supabase, Ireland | The account holder; us, for support | Until the account is deleted |
| Answer to "may we count usage?", with a random number for that visit if yes | Supabase | Us | 36 months |
| Usage counts: which buttons, no person | Supabase | Us | 26 months |
| A scrambled (salted hash) form of the internet address, for rate limits | Supabase | Us | 1 day |
| Error reports: message, screen, browser type | Supabase | Us | 30 days |
| Feedback emails sent to us | Our mailbox | Us | 2 years after the last reply |

Processors: Supabase (database, Ireland), Vercel (hosting, EU and USA), Resend (email, USA, only when alert emails are on). No AI processes anyone's data. No supplier or installer receives anything. The retention periods are enforced by `purge_expired()`, run daily.

## Is it necessary and proportionate?

- A meter file gives the closest figures, but nobody has to load one: the app works from answers alone.
- Readings stay on the person's device unless they make an account, and accounts are off for the test.
- Usage counts are asked for, refusing is as easy as accepting, and they carry no identity.
- We keep the least that lets the app work and lets us fix faults, each for a stated time.

## Risks to people, and what we do about them

| Risk | Likelihood, severity | What we do | Left |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meter readings show when a home is empty | Low, serious | Readings never leave the device without an account; accounts are off for the test; when on, each account is readable only by its holder (row-level security) | Low |
| A stolen or guessed password opens an account | Low, moderate | Accounts off for the test. Before they go on: leaked-password protection, minimum 8 characters | Low |
| Someone reads data through the database's public interface | Low, serious | Row-level security on every table; the installer portal's functions are closed while the portal is off (supabase/portal_locked_2026_10.sql) | Low |
| Data kept longer than we say | Medium, moderate | `purge_expired()` deletes on schedule; the privacy page states each period | Low |
| Wrong figures lead someone to a worse plan or a poor solar purchase | Medium, moderate (money, not privacy) | Figures say they are estimates; prices are re-read from the suppliers' own pages daily; a plan the check cannot read for 7 days is marked unverified, and the app says so when it recommends that plan | Medium: the test exists to find these |
| Transfers to the USA | Low, low | Vercel and Resend under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses | Low |
| A feedback email holds more than the person meant to share | Low, low | The email is pre-filled with the screen and build only; the person writes the rest | Low |

## Before the public launch

1. Data processing terms of Supabase, Vercel and Resend accepted in the company's name.
2. Leaked-password protection on, and Supabase on a plan with backups, before accounts are switched on.
3. A solicitor's review of the privacy notice, terms and this assessment.
4. This assessment reviewed again if anything above changes: accounts on, quote requests on, a new processor, or AI on anyone's data.
