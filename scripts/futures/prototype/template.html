20-year gain = bill savings over 20 years minus the system's price after the SEAI grant, in today's money (future savings discounted 3% a year). Savings are measured against the cheapest plan you could be on without solar. Export income above €400 a year is taxed at 40%. Prices use V8's 2026 install guide prices (mid-market), not quotes. "Your system" is 10 panels at 460 W plus 9 kWh, with the benchmark price. The 8 futures phase in over five years and are weighted equally. The battery is modelled with a 5 kW charge limit and simple rules, so smart controls such as a SigenStor may do better than shown.
