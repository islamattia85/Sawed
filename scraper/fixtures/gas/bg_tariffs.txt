There’s no Estimated Annual Bill (EAB) for this plan. That’s because day‑ahead market prices change every half hour, so there isn’t a fixed annual cost that can be accurately calculated upfront.
There’s also no discount applied to the unit rate. With the Smart Dynamic tariff, you’re already getting direct access to wholesale electricity prices.
