Understanding dual fuel
What are dual fuel tariffs and how do they work?
Dual fuel tariffs combine your electricity and gas supply under one plan with a single energy provider. Instead of managing two separate suppliers, you get one account and one point of contact, which 
Will I get one electricity bill with a dual fuel plan?
No, you’ll still receive separate electricity and gas bills, but they’re available in one place — online or in our app — with one billing period, one login and one customer service team.
Can I stay with separate suppliers for gas and electricity?
Yes, but combining your fuels with one provider through a dual fuel contract can offer benefits like simpler billing, easier support and access to exclusive dual fuel discounts.
Are dual fuel deals always the cheapest option?

Can I use dual fuel plans with electric vehicles?
Yes. Some dual fuel tariffs include EV-friendly features like off-peak hours for charging at reduced rates. Our Smart EV Dual Fuel Discount plan is designed with electric vehicles in mind.
Making a decision
How can I find a cheaper deal for my home?
Compare unit rates, standing charges and any applicable discount periods. The cheapest deal may vary by electricity supply region, meter type and whether you’re eligible for a smart plan.
What usage details do I need to compare plans?
When switching plans, it helps to know your annual consumption, average usage and current plan details. These figures can be found on recent bills or by checking your online account.
How do average household energy costs compare?
Based on average consumption figures (11,000 kWh gas and 4,200 kWh electricity per year), typical annual consumption can range from €2,000 to €2,500, depending on the plan and provider.
What’s included in a dual fuel discount?
A dual fuel discount typically reduces your electricity or gas charges when both are supplied by the same provider. Discounts may apply during a fixed discount period or across your full contract term
Comparing and switching
What is a variable tariff and how does it work?
A variable tariff means your unit rates may change over time based on the market. These plans can offer flexibility, but your rates may rise or fall depending on the Irish energy market.
How do contract terms affect pricing?
Shorter contract terms may offer more flexibility, while longer ones may come with better rates or discounts. Always check if early exit fees apply before switching from your current plan.
How do energy suppliers calculate energy costs?
Energy costs are based on your usage (in kilowatt hours), the unit rates for electricity and gas, and daily standing charges. Some energy providers also factor in whether you’re using a variable rate 
What’s the difference between energy suppliers and electricity suppliers?
An electricity supplier only provides electricity, while energy suppliers may offer both electricity and gas. Dual fuel energy providers, like Bord Gáis Energy, give you the option to combine both.
Billing and account management

Does paperless billing affect my plan?
Many energy suppliers offer incentives or small savings when you opt for paperless billing. It’s also a more sustainable way to manage your account.
What are standing charges and unit rates?
Standing charges are daily fixed fees for maintaining your connection, while unit rates are the price you pay per kilowatt hour of energy used. Both impact your final electricity and gas bills.
Energy use and efficiency
How is my energy usage measured?
Your energy usage is measured in kilowatt hours (kWh). Most customers have either a regular meter or smart meter, and usage is tracked for both electricity and gas consumption.
